PRIVACY POLICY
Pirkoo

What personal data Pirkoo handles (the app at app.pirkoo.com, the WooCommerce plugin that connects your store to it, and the website pirkoo.com), why, who processes it for us and how long we keep it. Pirkoo is made by tvarkom.

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Who we are
----------

The data controller is MB TVARKOM SOFTWARE ("tvarkom", "we", "us"), a small partnership (mažoji bendrija) registered in Lithuania under company code 308171668, registered office Gedimino g. 22A-14, 44319 Kaunas, Lithuania. For privacy questions and data requests, email privacy@pirkoo.com.

Your Pirkoo account
-------------------

* Logging in: you log in to app.pirkoo.com with your email address. We email you a one-time login link and keep the address, the time and your IP address with the request, to limit how many links can be asked for. When you use the link, we create your account (your email address, when you joined and last logged in, and the language you use the app in: English or Brazilian Portuguese, taken from your browser at your first login and changed in the account menu) and a session, kept in one cookie (below). The app and its emails to you are in that language.

* Companies: an account belongs to companies, and a company holds stores. We keep each company's name, its people and their roles (Owner, Admin, Member or Viewer), and whether you've used your free trial.

* Invitations: when someone in a company invites you, we keep your email address, the role offered, who invited you and when, and email you the invitation. You don't need an account to receive one.

* The session cookie: app.pirkoo.com sets one cookie, the session (HttpOnly, Secure, valid 30 days), and a short-lived one that remembers the page you were opening before logging in. Both are strictly necessary to use the app. There are no analytics, advertising or third-party cookies.

* Usage records: we record how you use the app (for example logins, stores connected and removed, scans run and dashboards viewed), linked to your account and company, to improve Pirkoo. They hold the app's own numbers for the account, company and store, the action and its time; never your IP address, browser, email address or store data. We keep them 13 months, then only monthly totals without any link to you. We use no analytics cookies or third-party analytics.

Connecting your store
---------------------

The Pirkoo plugin contacts nobody until an administrator clicks "Connect to Pirkoo" on the store's Plugins screen and confirms on app.pirkoo.com, where what is collected is listed first. Connecting sends your browser to app.pirkoo.com with the store's address, the plugin's address on it and a one-time code; app.pirkoo.com then asks the plugin for a connection secret, which we keep encrypted. From then on, the plugin answers only requests signed with that secret.

Scans
-----

A scan runs when someone in the store's company starts one in the app, and once a day on its own, between 02:00 and 05:00 in the store's timezone (skipped when a scan ran in the 12 hours before; a store can be scanned at most 30 times a day). For each scan app.pirkoo.com asks the plugin for the store's data, and the plugin sends:

* About the store: its home, cart and checkout page addresses, the site's admin email (WordPress Settings → General, as the store's contact address; we send no marketing to it), the store's name, timezone, date and time formats and language, the titles the store gives its enabled payment and shipping methods (never their settings), and the folder names of its installed plugins and whether each is active (to show which of the plugins we recommend it already has).

* Store data: PHP, WordPress and WooCommerce versions; database, options table and autoloaded options sizes; the number of active plugins and the object cache type; background task (Action Scheduler) counts and how late WP-Cron runs; the number of orders in the last 30 days as a range (for example 50–200); the names and sizes of the largest autoloaded options and of database tables left by removed plugins; PHP settings such as the memory limit; failed and late background task names, with error messages stripped of email addresses, links, IP addresses and long numbers; and a profile of one checkout page load on your store's server, with a temporary cart that is never saved: plugin, theme and hook names with their timings, database query shapes with every value replaced by "?", the host names of outside services called, and object cache hit and miss counts. Option, table, task, hook and host names are chosen by plugins; before they're sent, the plugin replaces any email address, run of six or more digits, or long hexadecimal or UUID identifier in them with a placeholder.

* Business context, so the analysis can explain its findings in business terms: your store's country and currency; the number of paid and on-hold orders in the last 30 days, and of failed, pending and cancelled orders; gross sales in the last 30 days and the average order value, as exact amounts in your store's currency; how many customers in the last 30 days were new and how many returning, as two counts; the number of published products and variations and whether stock management is on; the IDs of the enabled payment gateways and shipping methods (never their settings or keys); whether checkout uses the Checkout block or the classic shortcode and whether High-Performance Order Storage is on; the database and web server product; how many of your theme's WooCommerce template overrides are outdated; how many plugins have an update waiting; the name of an active backup plugin, if any; the domain your store's emails come from (never the address); how many products were added to the cart, checkout pages opened and orders placed over the last 30 days, which the plugin counts on your store's server as daily totals, without cookies, browser scripts or anything about the shopper, keeping 35 days and deleting them when you uninstall it; and how many orders in the last 30 days came from each source type and from the 10 most common campaign sources, from WooCommerce Order Attribution (a campaign name that looks like an email address, a long number or an identifier is counted as "other", never sent).

* Site checks: whether a WordPress or theme update is waiting, whether debug output is shown, whether search engines are asked not to index the store, the site's WordPress environment type, how many database tables use the MyISAM engine or a character set other than utf8mb4, how many expired transients are stored, how many administrator accounts exist (a count, no user details) and whether the dashboard file editor is on; the upload limit, missing PHP extensions, free and total disk space, the server's load, CPU core count and memory; how many plugins and themes WordPress paused, how many fatal errors the store's PHP error log shows for the last 7 days (only the count, never log lines), the automatic update settings and whether permalinks are plain; the database's response time and how many spam comments, auto-drafts and old revisions it holds; how many core files differ from WordPress.org's official checksums, which the plugin fetches from api.wordpress.org sending only the WordPress version and language; wp-config.php's file permissions, whether a user named "admin" exists (yes or no), and whether registration is open and the role new accounts get.

For a scan, the plugin doesn't read customers' names, email addresses or postal addresses, order contents, option contents or task arguments, and sends sales only as the two 30-day totals above. The store's orders and customers reach us only through the store records below. We keep the store's latest name, timezone, formats, language, method titles and plugin list in the app to show its pages in the store's own way.

What the scan reads. Our scanner loads your store's public homepage, cart and checkout pages the way a visitor would, without running their JavaScript: it measures response times, page size and caching, and reads response headers and public platform markers. It also checks your HTTPS certificate and your domain's public SPF and DMARC records, and requests 18 well-known addresses on your store (such as configuration backups, .env files, the debug log, a .git folder, database dumps, site archives and phpinfo pages) to see whether anyone could download them; it reads at most 4 KB of each answer, follows no redirect and keeps only how many were found, never their contents. It doesn't log in, submit forms, place orders or read anything that isn't public.

Your scans' analysis. We keep each scan's full analysis, including your store's business context and its sales amounts, linked to your store for 12 months after the scan. We use it to show it to the people in your store's company on app.pirkoo.com and to answer you when you contact us. For a sole trader, sales can relate to a person: we keep them only for this, and they're not part of our statistics across stores.

Your audit history. For each completed scan we keep: the health score and risk level; which checks found a problem and how many points each cost; the measurements (such as server, cart and checkout response times, page weight and number of files, the autoloaded options size, the PHP version and object cache type, the days left on your HTTPS certificate, and whether SPF and DMARC records were found). It's linked to your store through the scan request, which holds the admin email until it's erased after 90 days. We use it to show your store's results over time, to answer you, and, across stores, to improve our scanner.

Your store's profile. We keep the latest business context of each store (not the analysis, the store data, the checkout profile or any sales amount), replaced by each scan. We use it as totals across stores, for example how many stores per country, size or checkout type, to understand which stores use Pirkoo, and to support your store when you contact us.

Who sees a store. Only the people in the company holding the store, on app.pirkoo.com. If two companies hold the same store (for example an agency and the shop owner), each sees the store's results and history.

Relay mode. When a store's firewall blocks our requests to the plugin, an administrator can connect it in relay mode: the plugin then calls app.pirkoo.com itself to fetch our requests and send their answers (every 5 minutes, and when we ask through the store's home page). The data, its purposes and its retention are the same.

Disconnecting and removing. Disconnecting a store (in the app or from its Plugins screen) stops its scans; its results and history stay in the company, and the company's Owners and Admins get an email when the store disconnects itself. Removing a store from a company deletes it from that company; when no other company holds it, its results, history, analyses and profile are deleted from our systems too.

Store records
-------------

From Pirkoo 0.4, so the people in a store's company can see its orders, refunds, customers, products and coupons on app.pirkoo.com, app.pirkoo.com asks the plugin for the store's records: the last 12 months when the store is connected, then what changed, once a day.

* Orders: number, status, dates (created, paid, completed), currency, totals (subtotal, discounts, shipping, tax, total), the payment method's title, the products and variations ordered with their quantities, prices and chosen attributes, the coupons used, and refunds with their amounts, items and reasons.

* The shopper on each order (the billing details): first and last name, email address, phone number, company, city, state, postcode and country. We don't receive the street address, shipping details, order notes, IP addresses or payment details.

* Registered customers: name, email address, phone number, city, state, postcode, country and when they registered.

* Products and variations: name, SKU, type, status, regular price and price, stock settings and quantity, categories, attributes, and when they were created and changed. Coupons: code, type, amount, dates and how many times used.

For the shoppers' data in these records, the store is the data controller and we process it on the store's behalf, under the store's instructions, only to show it to the people in the store's company on app.pirkoo.com: we don't contact shoppers, combine their data across stores, use it for our statistics or share it with anyone but the providers below. Stores that need a data processing agreement can ask for one at privacy@pirkoo.com. Shoppers with a request about their data should contact the store; we help the store answer it.

We keep orders and their refunds for 12 months after the order was placed; customers, products and coupons for as long as the store is connected and they exist on the store. Removing the store from its last company, or deleting the company, deletes them all.

WordPress management and monitoring
-----------------------------------

From Pirkoo 0.4, app.pirkoo.com shows each connected store's WordPress, plugins, themes and PHP errors, its known vulnerabilities, uptime, performance and broken links.

* Inventory: once a day and when someone opens these pages, the plugin sends WordPress's version and the newest one offered, each installed plugin's and theme's name, folder, version, author, whether it's active, the update offered and whether it updates on its own, and the PHP errors of the last 7 days in the store's PHP log, grouped (level, message, file and line, count, last time), with email addresses, IP addresses, links and long numbers removed on the store before they're sent. We keep the latest inventory, and a log of the updates started from the app (who, what, from and to which version, the outcome), while the store is in a company.

* Updates: when an Owner or Admin clicks Update in the app, the plugin updates that plugin, theme or WordPress as Dashboard → Updates does. Nothing is activated, deactivated or deleted from the app.

* Vulnerabilities: we download Wordfence Intelligence's public vulnerability list once a day and compare it with the inventory on our servers. Nothing about your store is sent to Wordfence.

* Uptime: every 5 minutes our servers in Amsterdam request your store's home page, as a visitor would, and record whether it answered, its HTTP status, response time and HTTPS certificate's expiry date. After 2 failed checks in a row, the company's Owners and Admins get an email, and another when the store is back.

* Performance: once a day we send your store's home page address (and nothing else) to Google PageSpeed Insights, which loads the page from Google's servers and returns its performance score and loading measurements, on mobile and desktop.

* Broken links: once a week our servers read up to 200 of your store's public pages, starting from the home page, and check every link and image on them; we keep the broken ones, with the page they're on. They never request cart, checkout, account, admin or add-to-cart addresses.

Billing
-------

A company's first 14 days are free; after that, scans need a paid plan. Plans are bought and managed on pages of our payment provider, Stripe: you enter your card or other payment details there, and we never see or keep them. Stripe gives us, and we keep for the company: its Stripe customer and subscription identifiers, the plan, the subscription's status and when it renews. Stripe keeps the billing contact, payment details and invoices. Invoices are issued through Stripe. We keep billing records for as long as accounting and tax law requires.

Legal basis (Art. 6 GDPR), by purpose
-------------------------------------

* Your account, companies, invitations, connecting stores, scanning them and showing their results, analysis and records to the company's people: providing the service you request (Art. 6(1)(b)). For shoppers' data in store records, we act as the store's processor (Art. 28 GDPR); the store's own legal basis applies. An invited person's email address: the inviting company's and our legitimate interest in letting it add its people (Art. 6(1)(f)).
* The audit history, the store profile and its statistics, the usage records, the login and scan limits, and IP addresses with login requests: our legitimate interests in supporting your store, showing its results over time, understanding how Pirkoo is used, improving it and preventing abuse (Art. 6(1)(f)).
* Billing: performing the contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) and complying with accounting and tax law (Art. 6(1)(c)).

You can object at any time to what rests on our legitimate interests: email privacy@pirkoo.com.

Pirkoo's website
----------------

pirkoo.com has no forms, analytics or cookies of its own. When you visit it, our hosting provider and Cloudflare process your IP address and the pages you request to deliver the site and protect it from abuse; if Cloudflare's bot protection checks your browser, it sets one security cookie, cf_clearance (HttpOnly, up to one year), which isn't used for tracking or advertising. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in running a secure website (Art. 6(1)(f)); the cookie is strictly necessary for that security. tvarkom's company website, tvarkom.com, has its own privacy notice there.

When you email us
-----------------

We keep your messages and contact details to answer you and, if you become a client, to do the work. Legal basis: taking steps at your request or performing a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)), or our legitimate interest in answering enquiries (Art. 6(1)(f)).

Who processes data for us
-------------------------

* Fly.io: hosts app.pirkoo.com, pirkoo.com, Pirkoo's API and our scanner, with their databases and logs, on servers in Amsterdam, Netherlands.

* Cloudflare: DNS for pirkoo.com and network protection for the website.

* Resend: sends the app's emails (login links, invitations, disconnection notices) from Ireland.

* Stripe: payments, subscriptions and invoices.

* Google: Google Workspace hosts our mailboxes; Google PageSpeed Insights tests your store's public home page (it receives only its address).

Some of these providers are based in the United States. Where data leaves the EU, they process it under an EU adequacy decision, such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, or Standard Contractual Clauses. We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.

How long we keep it
-------------------

These periods are applied automatically by our software:

* Login links (email address, IP address, time): 24 hours. Sessions: 30 days, or until you log out.

* Invitations: 30 days after they're accepted or expire (an invitation expires after 7 days).

* Your account: until you delete it (in the app's account menu), with your memberships and sessions. A company: until an Owner deletes it, with its stores, invitations and people's access; deleting a company also cancels its subscription.

* Stores in a company, with their connection secret and latest details: until they're removed or the company is deleted.

* Store records: orders and refunds, 12 months after the order was placed; customers, products and coupons, while the store is connected and they exist on it. All of them are deleted when the store is removed or its company deleted.

* WordPress inventory and update log: while the store is in a company. Uptime checks: 30 days; uptime incidents: 12 months after they ended. Performance tests: 12 months. Broken links: the store's last 5 weekly checks.

* Scan requests (store, page addresses and time): 24 months. The admin email in them is erased after 90 days. Failed scans, with any store data sent with them: 15 days after they failed.

* The store's contact email at our API: deleted after 12 months without a scan; the store's record there, 24 months after its last scan.

* Audit results (the audit history) and each scan's full analysis (with its business context and sales amounts): 12 months after each scan. They're also deleted with their scan request, or when the store is removed from the last company holding it.

* Store profiles: replaced by each scan, and deleted with the store's record at our API.

* Usage records: 13 months, then monthly totals only.

* Billing records: as long as accounting and tax law requires.

Other copies:

* Our mailboxes hold the emails you send us. They have no automatic deletion: we keep these messages for as long as we need them to deal with your request and any follow-up, and longer only where the law requires it.

* Database snapshots: our hosting provider takes daily snapshots of our databases, plus one before some updates, and deletes each after 5 days. Data we've deleted can remain in a snapshot until it expires. We use snapshots only to restore our service, and if we restore one, we delete again anything that had already been deleted.

* Logs: our service logs record store addresses and technical identifiers, not scan contents, email addresses or login links. Our hosting provider keeps them for about 7 days.

Your rights
-----------

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct or delete it, to restrict or object to how we use it, or to receive it in a portable format. Email privacy@pirkoo.com and we'll reply within one month. You can delete your account yourself in the app, and a company's Owner can remove stores or delete the company. For help with Pirkoo, email support@pirkoo.com.

You can also complain to the Lithuanian supervisory authority, the State Data Protection Inspectorate (vdai.lrv.lt (https://vdai.lrv.lt/en/)), or to the authority where you live.

Changes
-------

This policy is published at https://pirkoo.com/privacy/ and shipped with the Pirkoo plugin. If we change how we handle your data, we'll update it and the date at the top, and tell the people with a Pirkoo account by email before a change that affects them takes effect.
