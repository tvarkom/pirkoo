What Pirkoo does
----------------

Pirkoo scans the WooCommerce stores your company connects, when someone in the company asks and once a day, and shows the results on app.pirkoo.com: a health score, the checks that cost points, the measurements, the store's profile and how the score changes over time. Our scanner loads your store's public pages like a visitor would, and the plugin sends data it reads on your store's server, including your store's business context and a profile of one checkout page load, as described in our Privacy Policy (https://pirkoo.com/privacy/, also shipped with the plugin). Neither the scanner nor the plugin changes anything on your store, apart from a small table of daily shop totals the plugin keeps.

Results describe your store at the moment of each scan, and can vary between scans with traffic, hosting and caching.
