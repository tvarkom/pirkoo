What Pirkoo does
----------------

For the WooCommerce stores your company connects, Pirkoo:

* scans them, when someone in the company asks and once a day, and shows a health score, the checks that cost points, the measurements, the store's profile and how the score changes over time;

* copies the store's orders, refunds, customers, products and coupons of the last 12 months, once a day, and shows them to the company's people;

* shows the store's WordPress version, plugins and themes with their updates, its PHP errors of the last 7 days and the known vulnerabilities of what is installed, and updates a plugin, a theme or WordPress when an Owner or Admin asks for it;

* checks every 5 minutes that the store answers and emails the company's Owners and Admins when it doesn't and when it's back, tests its home page's performance once a day, and looks for broken links once a week.

Our scanner and monitors load your store's public pages like a visitor would. The plugin sends data it reads on your store's server, including the store's business context, a profile of one checkout page load and the records above. What is collected, why, who else processes it and how long it's kept is described in our Privacy Policy (https://pirkoo.com/privacy/, also shipped with the plugin), which is part of how the service works.

On your store, the plugin keeps its own settings, a small table of daily shop totals and short-lived working values, and for the checkout profile it uses a temporary cart that is never saved. It changes nothing else, except that it updates a plugin, a theme or WordPress when an Owner or Admin of your company starts that update in the app. Uninstalling the plugin removes what it kept.

Results describe your store at the moment of each scan or check, and can vary with traffic, hosting and caching.
